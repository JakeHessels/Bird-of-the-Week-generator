Bird of Week 45, 2025 
This weeks bird of the week is the Banded Quail (Philortyx fasciatus), a galliformes from the odontophoridae (new world quail) family.
native to: arid western Mexico (southwestern Jalisco to southeastern Guerrero, Morelos, and Puebla).
Conservation status: Extant.

Preview – Bird of Week 46, 2025 
Next weeks bird of the week will be the Mindoro Hornbill (Penelopides mindorensis), a bucerotiformes from the bucerotidae (hornbills) family.
native to: Mindoro (central Philippines).
Conservation status: Extant.
