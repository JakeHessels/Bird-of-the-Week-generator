Bird of Week 46, 2025 
This weeks bird of the week is the Mindoro Hornbill (Penelopides mindorensis), a bucerotiformes from the bucerotidae (hornbills) family.
native to: Mindoro (central Philippines).
Conservation status: Extant.

Preview – Bird of Week 47, 2025 
Next weeks bird of the week will be the Green Malkoha (Ceuthmochares australis), a cuculiformes from the cuculidae (cuckoos) family.
native to: Ethiopia to Kenya, Tanzania, Mozambique, and eastern South Africa.
Conservation status: Extant.
