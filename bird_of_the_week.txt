Bird of Week 32, 2025 
This weeks bird of the week is the Stripe-cheeked Woodpecker (Piculus callopterus), a piciformes from the picidae (woodpeckers) family.
native to: humid lowlands and foothills on both slopes of Panama.
Conservation status: Extant.

Preview – Bird of Week 33, 2025 
Next weeks bird of the week will be the Moorea Reed Warbler (Acrocephalus longirostris), a passeriformes from the acrocephalidae (reed warblers and allies) family.
native to: Moorea (Society Islands); probably extinct, last confirmed report in 1981.
Conservation status: Extant.
