Bird of Week 19, 2026 
This weeks bird of the week is the Rothschild's Swift (Cypseloides rothschildi), a apodiformes from the apodidae (swifts) family.
native to: Andes of northwestern Argentina; rarely adjacent southern Bolivia and Peru (Cusco).
Conservation status: Extant.
