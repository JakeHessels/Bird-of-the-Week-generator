Bird of Week 47, 2025 
This weeks bird of the week is the Green Malkoha (Ceuthmochares australis), a cuculiformes from the cuculidae (cuckoos) family.
native to: Ethiopia to Kenya, Tanzania, Mozambique, and eastern South Africa.
Conservation status: Extant.

Preview – Bird of Week 48, 2025 
Next weeks bird of the week will be the European Bee-eater (Merops apiaster), a coraciiformes from the meropidae (bee-eaters) family.
native to: southern Palearctic; winters to sub-Saharan Africa and western India.
Conservation status: Extant.
