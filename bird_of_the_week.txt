Bird of Week 24, 2026 
This weeks bird of the week is the Townsend's Shearwater (Puffinus auricularis), a procellariiformes from the procellariidae (shearwaters and petrels) family.
native to: breeds Revillagigedo Islands (off western Mexico); disperses to 8°N.
Conservation status: Extant.

Preview – Bird of Week 25, 2026 
Next weeks bird of the week will be the Helmeted Pygmy-Tyrant (Lophotriccus galeatus), a passeriformes from the tyrannidae (tyrant flycatchers) family.
native to: eastern Colombia to southern Venezuela, the Guianas, and northern Amazonian Brazil.
Conservation status: Extant.
