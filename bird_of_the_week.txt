Bird of Week 37, 2026 
This weeks bird of the week is the Pale-eyed Blackbird (Agelasticus xanthophthalmus), a passeriformes from the icteridae (troupials and allies) family.
native to: locally in marshes of eastern Ecuador and eastern Peru.
Conservation status: Extant.

Preview – Bird of Week 38, 2026 
Next weeks bird of the week will be the Bernier's Teal (Anas bernieri), a anseriformes from the anatidae (ducks, geese, and waterfowl) family.
native to: very local, lowlands of western Madagascar, mainly the coastal northwest.
Conservation status: Extant.
