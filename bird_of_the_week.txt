Bird of Week 25, 2026 
This weeks bird of the week is the Helmeted Pygmy-Tyrant (Lophotriccus galeatus), a passeriformes from the tyrannidae (tyrant flycatchers) family.
native to: eastern Colombia to southern Venezuela, the Guianas, and northern Amazonian Brazil.
Conservation status: Extant.
