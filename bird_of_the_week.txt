Bird of Week 9, 2026 
This weeks bird of the week is the Spectacled Spiderhunter (Arachnothera flavigaster), a passeriformes from the nectariniidae (sunbirds and spiderhunters) family.
native to: Thai-Malay Peninsula, Sumatra, and Borneo.
Conservation status: Extant.
