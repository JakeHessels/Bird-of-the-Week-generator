Bird of Week 42, 2025 
This weeks bird of the week is the Palau Fruit-Dove (Ptilinopus pelewensis), a columbiformes from the columbidae (pigeons and doves) family.
native to: Palau (Babelthuap to Angaur; western Caroline Islands, western Micronesia).
Conservation status: Extant.

Preview – Bird of Week 43, 2025 
Next weeks bird of the week will be the Dusky Nightjar (Antrostomus saturatus), a caprimulgiformes from the caprimulgidae (nightjars and allies) family.
native to: montane forest of Costa Rica and western Panama.
Conservation status: Extant.
