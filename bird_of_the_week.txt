Bird of Week 7, 2026 
This weeks bird of the week is the White-throated Robin (Irania gutturalis), a passeriformes from the muscicapidae (old world flycatchers) family.
native to: Türkiye, southern Caucasus, and Levant eastward to Iran, and from southern Kazakhstan and Tajikistan to western and northern Afghanistan; possibly also central Uzbekistan; winters in northeastern and eastern Africa.
Conservation status: Extant.
