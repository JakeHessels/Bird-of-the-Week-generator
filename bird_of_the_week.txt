Bird of Week 12, 2026 
This weeks bird of the week is the Fulvous-chinned Nunlet (Nonnula sclateri), a galbuliformes from the bucconidae (puffbirds) family.
native to: tropical southeastern Peru to northern Bolivia and western Brazil south of the Amazon.
Conservation status: Extant.

Preview – Bird of Week 13, 2026 
Next weeks bird of the week will be the Toro Olive-Greenbul (Phyllastrephus hypochloris), a passeriformes from the pycnonotidae (bulbuls) family.
native to: southern South Sudan, eastern Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda, far western Kenya, and far northwestern Tanzania.
Conservation status: Extant.
