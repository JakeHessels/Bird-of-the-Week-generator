Bird of Week 48, 2025 
This weeks bird of the week is the European Bee-eater (Merops apiaster), a coraciiformes from the meropidae (bee-eaters) family.
native to: southern Palearctic; winters to sub-Saharan Africa and western India.
Conservation status: Extant.
